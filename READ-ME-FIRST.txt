No database or Edge Function changes for this. (If you have not yet done the last round - migration 0040 and the two subscription functions - do those first.)

What changed: a VIN lookup button in the menu, right after Customers & units. Type or scan a VIN: it is checked for typos, decoded from the free NHTSA database (year, make, model, GVWR, engine, horsepower, axles, brakes, fuel, transmission, where it was built), matched against your own units (with a link to repair history), and checked for safety recalls. "Add as a new unit" fills the unit form for you. Recent lookups are remembered on that computer.
