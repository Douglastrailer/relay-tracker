RelayFleet website - VIN lookup for everyone

Upload ALL files to GitHub and commit. Refresh with Cmd+Shift+R. On phones, close Relay and reopen it.
No database or Edge Function changes.

What changed: VIN lookup is now available to every account.
- Shop staff: VIN lookup in the menu (as before).
- Admin: a VIN lookup tab in the admin menu.
- Fleet customers: a VIN lookup tab in the fleet portal menu.
- Mechanics: a VIN lookup button on their screen; it opens in the slide-up sheet.
Shop staff also see whether the VIN is one of their units and can add it; everyone sees the vehicle details and safety recalls.
The "Free, from the U.S. NHTSA vehicle database" line under the heading has been removed.
