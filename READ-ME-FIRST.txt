What changed:
- A customer who submits a request with an email gets a message when it is received, when it is approved (they can go ahead), and when it is declined WITH the reason.
- Declining needs a reason. Common reasons can be tapped; the customer reads what is written.
- Admin > Subscriptions: each shop shows its price, paid-through date, active or stopped, months paid, total paid and owed, in separate columns. Stop and Start keep history.
- Each shop has a billing sheet: every invoice, View (digital), PDF, Edit (figures lock once paid, note stays editable), Mark paid, Email, Void.
- Admin > Settings: every subscription invoice across all shops, filterable by status and shop, and Relays own details printed on invoices.
- Invoice emails now carry the PDF attached.
