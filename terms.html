6. Location tracking

Relay can show a mechanic's location to their own shop while they are on a road call. Mechanics turn this on themselves, and it stops when they mark the job finished or turn it off. Phones pause location when the app is closed or the screen is locked, so it is not continuous. Shops must tell their mechanics that this is in use, and must comply with the employment and privacy law that applies to them.
