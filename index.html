Questions shops ask

What is Relay?

Relay is shop and road service software for truck and trailer repair. Every repair lives on one work order — from the call or request, through dispatch, the mechanic on site, the estimate and the customer's approval, to the invoice and payment — and your office, your mechanics and your fleet customers all see the same job, live.

Who is it for?

Truck and trailer repair shops, mobile mechanics and roadside service businesses — from a two-mechanic shop with no dispatcher to companies running several locations.

Do mechanics need special devices?

No. Mechanics use Relay on their own phones (iPhone or Android). They accept road calls, navigate, run timers, add parts and photos, and can use voice commands. Relay can be installed on the home screen like an app — no app store needed.

How does live tracking work?

When a mechanic is on a road call and sharing location, the office sees them on the dispatch map and the fleet customer sees the ETA. Mechanics control when they share, and location is only visible to their own shop and the customer on that job. Phones pause location while an app is closed or the screen is locked, so the ETA updates while Relay is open on the mechanic's phone.

Can our fleet customers use it?

Yes. Fleet customers get their own portal: they request service, follow the mechanic's ETA, approve estimates, see photos and invoices, and look up every unit's repair history.

We use Fullbay, Shopmonkey or spreadsheets today. Can we bring our data?

Yes. Export your customers, vehicles and parts as CSV or Excel files and add them all at once — Relay recognises each file, matches the columns, and imports everything in the right order. Nothing is duplicated.

Does it work with QuickBooks?

Relay exports your customers, invoices, payments, products and vendors in the format QuickBooks Online imports.

Can we run more than one location?

Yes. Each location keeps its own customers, work orders, invoices, stock and mechanics, with its own name and details on its documents. The owner switches between them in a tap and compares them side by side in Reports.

Is our data private?

Each shop's data is kept separate and is only visible to that shop's team, with access by role (owner, office staff, mechanic) — and to fleet customers only for their own vehicles and jobs.

How do we get started?

Book a demo call and we will walk you through Relay with your own kind of work. Or set up your company yourself in a few minutes, invite your mechanics with your shop code, and add or import your customers.
