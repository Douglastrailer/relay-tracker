Run your shop floor and your road calls from one screen.

Relay is shop management and dispatch software for truck and trailer repair. Track every job in the bay on one workflow board, send mechanics out on road calls and see them on the map, and invoice from the finished job.

How a job moves through Relay

  1. The request comes in

    Your customer fills out your request link: their unit, the problem, and either where they broke down or when they can bring it in. No account needed. Or you create the job yourself.

  2. You assign a mechanic

    In-shop jobs go straight onto the board. For a road call, the mechanic goes live and you see them on the map, and so does your fleet customer.

  3. The work gets documented

    Status moves across the board as the job progresses. Chat and before/after photos stay attached to the job.

  4. The invoice goes out

    Send a branded PDF invoice from the finished job, mark it paid, and ask for a review.

Live GPS when the job is on the road

When a mechanic heads out to a breakdown, they go live from their phone and you follow them on the map, with directions to the exact location. Fleet customers see the same progress in their own account, so nobody has to call you for an update. Jobs in the bay don't use GPS at all.

Your data stays yours

Every company gets its own private workspace. Jobs, customers, prices, and invoices are separated at the database level, so no other business on Relay can see them. You decide who joins your team, and a deactivated account loses access immediately.
