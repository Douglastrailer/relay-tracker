Customer can follow along

One work order, start to paid.

Nobody re-types anything. Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. RequestA customer sends your request link, or you open a work order yourself.
  2. EstimateDiagnosis, labor and parts — sent for approval with one click.
  3. ApprovalThe customer approves on their phone. You see it instantly.
  4. RepairTimers, parts and photos from the mechanic's phone, in the bay or on the road.
  5. InvoiceBuilt from the approved estimate and the real work. Sent as a PDF.
  6. PaidRecord payments, full or partial. The history stays with the unit.

Everyone gets the view they need.

The office

A shop board from new to invoiced, dispatch with live mechanic locations, estimates, invoices, inventory and purchase orders. Exports for QuickBooks Online.

Mechanics

Their jobs on their phone. Accept a road call, navigate, run the timer, add parts and photos, clock in and out. Nothing they don't need.

Fleet customers

Where the mechanic is and when they'll arrive. Estimates to approve, invoices, and every unit's repair history — without calling to ask.
